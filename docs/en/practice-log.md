# Practice log

[简体中文](../实践记录.md) | **English**

This page records facts and conclusions from trials. No trial results have been recorded yet. A documented article is not evidence that its recommendations work.

## Starting a trial

1. Choose a specific backlog problem and describe current behavior and available evidence.
2. Define scope, owner, duration, and observations. Avoid changing too many factors at once.
3. Create a separate record with the [retrospective template](../../templates/en/practice-retrospective.md) and link an Issue.
4. Compare results with costs, including other factors that may have affected the outcome.
5. Decide what to keep, adjust, or stop, and update the relevant article.

Only publish evidence that complies with the [privacy policy](../../PRIVACY.en.md).

## Record index

After an actual trial, add a dated link, related topic, status, and one-sentence conclusion here. State source limitations, sample scope, and measurement definitions in the individual record.
